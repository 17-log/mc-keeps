## Original project artwork and personality content

`persona-art.js` contains original 32 × 32 food pixel matrices and expression variants used by McKeeps for SVG display and Canvas export. The eight food categories, sixteen life-preference role combinations, questions, copy and journal tasks are independently implemented for this project. The QR library generates QR patterns only; it is not the source of the pixel characters.

No character art, stickers, screenshots, questionnaire text or result copy from Sipor, SBTI, MBTI / 16Personalities or the referenced social-media examples is included as a personality asset. Research links in [docs/personality.md](docs/personality.md) identify inspiration and context, not an asset license or endorsement.
